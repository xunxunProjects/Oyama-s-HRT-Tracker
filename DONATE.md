# Donate

Oyama's HRT Tracker is free: no subscriptions, no paid features, no ads.
Donations are **optional and never required**, and they never unlock or add
anything in the app. They only give the maintainers a bit of motivation to
keep going.

## Why we ask

Running the project costs real money: hosting the public instance, the
domain, and the AI calls used during development and testing. If funding
stays short for a long time, development will slow down.

## Where the money goes

- 40% — project development and maintenance (infrastructure, releases, reviews)
- 30% — [@fantuanmtf](https://github.com/fantuanmtf)
- 30% — [@SmirnovaOyama](https://github.com/SmirnovaOyama)
